About Our CSGO Polygon Mirror Guide

csgopolygonmirror.com is an informational guide that helps Counter-Strike 2 players find official CSGO Polygon mirrors and avoid phishing copies. The site publishes the current PLG.BET domain list, explains how mirrors work and shows how to protect a Steam account.

csgopolygonmirror.com is not PLG.BET and is not an official mirror. The site does not host games, process Steam logins or accept deposits. Pages include links to PLG.BET so readers can reach the official site directly.

What the Site Covers

csgopolygonmirror.com covers five topics, each on its own page:

Mirror list

The current CSGO Polygon mirror list with check dates, on the home page.

Access problems

Fixes for a mirror that does not open, on Mirror Not Working.

Phishing copies

Warning signs of phishing copies, on Fake CSGO Polygon Mirrors.

Safety facts

Account, license and reputation facts, on Is a CSGO Polygon Mirror Safe.

Everyday use

Login, deposit and promo code basics, on How to Use a CSGO Polygon Mirror.

Our Editors

Alex Voss and Daniel Kerr are pen names. The two editors behind them write and check every page on csgopolygonmirror.com. The editors use pen names to avoid targeted phishing and harassment, which is common in the CS2 skin trading community.

Alex Voss, Editor

Mirror guides and weekly checks

Alex Voss writes the mirror guides and runs the weekly domain checks described on How We Check Mirrors. Alex focuses on Steam account security: Steam OpenID, Steam Guard, Web API key scams and Trade Protection. Alex updates the mirror list within 24 hours of a new post in the Telegram channel @plgbet.

Daniel Kerr, Research Editor

Fact-checking and sources

Daniel Kerr fact-checks every page before publication and before each update. Daniel checks each claim against its source: official PLG.BET channels, Steam Community pages, regulator validators and published reviews. Daniel adds the source and the check date to every figure on the site.

Editorial Policy

csgopolygonmirror.com follows five editorial rules:

  1. 1

    Every mirror domain comes from an official PLG.BET channel, never from search results or messages.

  2. 2

    Every figure carries a named source and a date.

  3. 3

    Negative facts stay on the page, including low review scores and licensing gaps.

  4. 4

    csgopolygonmirror.com does not publish promo codes, deposit minimums or bonus values that the editors cannot verify.

  5. 5

    Pages show a "Last updated" date, and corrections are logged on How We Check Mirrors.

Contact the Editors

Readers can send corrections, questions and reports of fake mirrors through the Contact page. The editors answer within 48 hours on business days.
