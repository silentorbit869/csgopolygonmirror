How We Check Every CSGO Polygon Mirror

csgopolygonmirror.com lists a CSGO Polygon mirror only after the domain passes four checks. The checks cover the official source, a live test, the certificate and the Steam login. A domain that fails one check stays off the mirror list.

The Four Checks for Every Mirror

01

Official source

The domain appears in the Telegram channel @plgbet or on X @PLGBET. Pass: listed in a post from the official account.

02

Live test

The domain opens from a clean browser. Pass: HTTP 200 on the domain or a redirect to a listed domain.

03

Certificate

The HTTPS certificate matches the domain. Pass: valid certificate, no browser warning.

04

Steam login

The sign-in button opens Steam OpenID. Pass: window on steamcommunity.com/openid.

The editorial team never adds a domain from search results, forums or direct messages. Passing checks 2 to 4 is not enough. An official PLG.BET source is the entry condition for the list.

How Often We Check

The editorial team checks every listed domain once a week. A new post in the Telegram channel @plgbet triggers a check within 24 hours. Each check produces one line in the status log below, with the date and the result.

Legacy domains are checked on the same schedule. A legacy domain stays on the site as plain text with its status. Players who search for the domain can see what happened to it.

What We Do Not Check

csgopolygonmirror.com checks domain access and login safety, not the operator's business. The editorial team does not test deposits, withdrawals, bonus terms or game fairness with real funds. The Is a CSGO Polygon Mirror Safe page explains the license, fairness and review checks for PLG.BET.

Corrections

Report a wrong status

Players who find a wrong status can report it through the Contact page. The editorial team rechecks the domain within 24 hours and adds a log line with the corrected result.
