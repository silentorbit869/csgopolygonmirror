What a CSGO Polygon Mirror Is

A CSGO Polygon mirror is an alternate domain of PLG.BET. The mirror runs the same platform, database and games under another address.

Gambling operators run mirrors for three practical reasons:

  • Internet providers in some countries block gambling domains at the DNS level.
  • Operators retire a domain after a block and move traffic to a new address.
  • A second domain keeps the PLG.BET platform reachable during outages on the main domain.

PLG.BET and an official mirror offer the same account and balance, while an unofficial copy offers neither. An unofficial copy exists to capture Steam credentials, so the difference matters more than the address itself.
