An official CSGO Polygon mirror carries the same risk level as PLG.BET itself. The mirror runs the same platform on another address. The real danger comes from unofficial copies that are missing from the Telegram channel @plgbet.

Short answers

  • Is the operator licensed? The main domain shows an Anjouan Gaming license seal (checked September 25, 2026)

Same Operator, License and Fairness

Every official CSGO Polygon mirror runs under the same operator, license and fairness system as PLG.BET.

Which License PLG.BET and Its Mirrors Share

The PLG.BET main domain loads a license seal from anjouangaming.org, the seal service of the Anjouan gaming regulator. The seal was present on September 25, 2026.

Players can check the license on PLG.BET's official site by opening the footer seal. A valid seal opens the regulator's validator page for that domain. The license check answers the question "is CSGO Polygon legit" at the operator level. Anjouan is an offshore regulator, so players get fewer dispute options than under a UK Gambling Commission license.

How Provably Fair Works on Every Mirror

Provably Fair is a verification system that lets a player confirm each game result with cryptographic seeds. The system works in four steps:

  1. 1

    Step 1The platform publishes a hash of its secret server seed before the round.

  2. 2

    Step 2The player sets or receives a client seed.

  3. 3

    Step 3The server seed, client seed and a nonce produce the round result.

  4. 4

    Step 4The platform reveals the server seed, and the player checks it against the earlier hash.

The same fairness data appears on PLG.BET and on each official mirror, since both show one account history. PLG.BET describes its games as provably fair on the main domain. A matching hash answers the question "is CSGO Polygon rigged" for each checked round.

Where the Real Risk Is

The real risk of a CSGO Polygon mirror sits in unofficial addresses and in operator terms. The official mirror itself adds no extra risk.

Unofficial copies steal Steam credentials, Web API keys and skins through fake login windows. The Fake CSGO Polygon Mirrors guide lists every warning sign and the recovery steps.

Operator risk

Operator risk stays the same on every domain. Players should read the PLG.BET terms on withdrawals, bonus rules and account restrictions before a first deposit.

What to Check in PLG.BET Player Reviews

Player reviews of PLG.BET are public on Trustpilot. Recent reviews show how the operator handles withdrawals and support tickets today.

Four topics deserve attention in any PLG.BET review:

  • How long withdrawals take after KYC.
  • Why accounts were restricted, if they were.
  • Whether promised bonuses were credited.
  • How fast support answered disputed tickets.

The same reviews apply to every official domain, because the mirror does not change how PLG.BET handles payouts.

Account Protection Checklist

Five settings protect a PLG.BET account on any CSGO Polygon mirror.

On the Steam side

  • Steam Guard Mobile Authenticator turned on for the Steam account.

On the platform side

  • Two-factor authentication turned on in PLG.BET account settings where the option is offered.

FAQs

Is it safe to use a PLG.BET mirror?

A PLG.BET mirror is as safe as the main domain when it appears in the Telegram channel @plgbet. The mirror shares one account, one balance and one set of terms with PLG.BET. Unlisted mirrors carry phishing risk.

Will my balance and history show on a CSGO Polygon mirror?

A CSGO Polygon mirror shows the same balance and bet history as PLG.BET, because every official domain reads one database. Players sign in with the same Steam account.

Is PLG.BET licensed?

PLG.BET displays an Anjouan Gaming license seal on its main domain, checked on September 25, 2026. Anjouan is an offshore regulator with fewer player protections than the UK Gambling Commission.

Is CSGO Polygon rigged?

CSGO Polygon results can be checked with Provably Fair data: the server seed hash, the client seed and the nonce. A revealed server seed that matches its earlier hash confirms the round was not changed.
