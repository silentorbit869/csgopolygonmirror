Responsible Gambling for CSGO Polygon Mirror Users

Gambling with CS2 skins carries the same financial risk as any casino. Most players lose money over time. csgopolygonmirror.com is for readers aged 18+ (21+ where local law requires it, including many US states).

Age and Local Law

Skin gambling is restricted or illegal in some countries. A site that accepts a sign-up does not prove that play is legal for that player. The legal age for online gambling is 18 in the UK and most EU countries. Many US states set it at 21. Players should check the law where they live before any deposit.

Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Problem gambling often starts slowly and goes unnoticed by the player. Five signs call for a pause:

  • Spending more money or skins than planned for a session.
  • Chasing losses with bigger bets or new deposits.
  • Selling or trading skins mainly to fund more gambling.
  • Hiding gambling from friends or family.
  • Feeling restless or irritable when not playing.

One of these signs is a reason to take a break. Several of them together are a reason to contact a free helpline listed below.

How to Set Limits

Clear limits set before a session protect a player's budget better than decisions made during play.

  1. 1

    Step 1Set a monthly budget in money, not in skins, and never raise it after a loss.

  2. 2

    Step 2Use deposit and session limits in the casino account settings where they are offered.

  3. 3

    Step 3Keep valuable skins in a separate Steam account that never connects to gambling sites.

  4. 4

    Step 4Stop at a fixed time and keep the session short.

Self-Exclusion and Blocking Tools

National self-exclusion schemes cover only operators licensed in that country. GAMSTOP in the UK and OASIS in Germany apply to UK and German licensed operators. Offshore casinos with an Anjouan license, such as PLG.BET, are not part of these schemes.

Blocking software works across all gambling sites on a device, including offshore ones. Gamban and BetBlocker block gambling domains on phones and computers. Players can also ask a casino's support team to close or self-exclude an account.

If Someone Else Is Affected

Family members and friends often notice problem gambling before the player does. The NCPG, GamCare and check-dein-spiel.de all offer advice for people close to a player. Talking about spending and time, not about blame, keeps the conversation open.
