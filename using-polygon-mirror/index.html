Deposit CS2 Skins

The answer to how to deposit skins on csgo polygon starts with a Steam trade URL. Step 1: Copy the trade URL from Steam under Inventory, Trade Offers, "Who can send me Trade Offers?". Step 2: Paste the trade URL into PLG.BET settings and set the Steam inventory to public. Step 3: Pick skins in the PLG.BET deposit window and confirm the trade in the Steam Mobile app.

Steam Trade Protection applies to Counter-Strike 2 items for 7 days after a trade, according to PC Gamer. Players should check the credit status of each skin in the PLG.BET deposit history before playing. Players should check that the account in the Steam trade window matches the bot shown on PLG.BET.

Players who prefer the main domain for payments can make the same deposit on PLG.BET itself. The balance appears on every official mirror once the deposit is credited.

Withdrawals on a CSGO Polygon Mirror

A csgo polygon payout works the same way on an official mirror as on PLG.BET. The methods and KYC rules stay the same.

Players withdraw skins through a Steam trade offer. PLG.BET can request KYC documents before a withdrawal under its terms. Limits, fees and processing times are set in the PLG.BET terms. Players should read them in the cashier before a first withdrawal.

Withdrawal speed depends on PLG.BET processing, not on the mirror address. Players should keep deposit and withdrawal records until each payout arrives.

FAQs

Do I need a new account for a CSGO Polygon mirror?

A CSGO Polygon mirror needs no new account. Players sign in with the same Steam profile through Steam OpenID and see the same PLG.BET balance and history.

Can I deposit skins through a CSGO Polygon mirror?

A CSGO Polygon mirror accepts CS2 skin deposits through the same Steam trade URL as PLG.BET. The skins land in one shared balance after the trade is confirmed in the Steam Mobile app.

Do PLG.BET promo codes work on mirrors?

PLG.BET promo codes work on every official mirror, because the code attaches to the account. Current codes appear in the Telegram channel @plgbet.

Why is my CSGO Polygon skin deposit pending?
